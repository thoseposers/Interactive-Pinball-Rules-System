Pool Balls and ReRack

Complete the drop targets to light the 8 ball drop target. Shooting the 8 ball target then collects a rack (Max 3) worth 50,000.

After collecting a rack, ReRack mode begins. Shoot the stand ups to complete ReRack and earn the bonus. Bonus begins at a maximum value and quickly counts down. Each shot to a lit ReRack letter resets the bonus to the maximum value.

Hitting a drop target scores 5000 and increases your Bonus. Hitting a stand up scores 3000.
